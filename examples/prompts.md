# Example prompts

These are examples of the kinds of instructions an AI agent could interpret for complex 3D output.

- "Design a futuristic chair made from smooth cast material with organic curves, soft stress transitions, and a premium matte finish."
- "Create a realistic rock outcrop with fractured surfaces, irregular topography, and natural color variation."
- "Generate a sculptural lamp with smooth, flowing edges and metallic detailing."
- "Model a creature-like object with asymmetrical plastic forms, layered textures, and subtle surface imperfections."
- "Build a dramatic vase with swelling curves and a hand-finished ceramic texture."
